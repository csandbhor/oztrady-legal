Support

OzTrady is job, task and stock software for trades businesses, with a phone app for the crew.

Can't sign in?

  • Use the email your business invited you with. On the sign-in page or in the app, tap Forgot password? to get a code by email.
  • Never got your invitation? On the sign-in page, enter your email and tap Resend my invitation email. Check your spam folder.
  • Your account is created and managed by your business. For access to more of the app, ask your business's owner or admin.

Remove my account

In the phone app (under Sign out) or on the website (My account), tap Request account removal. Your business's owner and admins are asked to remove your account and personal details.

Contact us

Email csandbhor@gmail.com. Please include your business name and what you were doing when the problem happened.
